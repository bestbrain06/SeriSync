Course Title

Sequences & Series in Real Life

A practical exploration of how arithmetic and geometric sequences and series appear in everyday situations, finance, population studies, and asset management.

Mathematics Topics
  • Arithmetic Sequences & Series
  • Geometric Sequences & Series
  • Summation Formulas (Sn)
  • Applications in Finance & Growth
  • Depreciation Models
Class Level: SHS 2

Senior High School

Designed for Grades 11–12 / equivalent secondary mathematics learners. Suitable for students who have completed basic algebra.

Lesson Objectives
  • Identify real-life situations that can be modelled by sequences and series
  • Apply arithmetic and geometric sequence formulas to savings and investment problems
  • Model population growth using geometric sequences
  • Calculate depreciation of assets over time using series
  • Solve practical problems and interpret mathematical results in context
