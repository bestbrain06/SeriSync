Saving and Investment Plans

Worked Example

Maria deposits $200 at the beginning of each year into a savings account that pays 5% interest per year, compounded annually. How much will she have after 4 years?

This is a geometric series. The amounts after interest are:

200(1.05)4 + 200(1.05)3 + 200(1.05)2 + 200(1.05)1

Using the sum of a geometric series Sn = a(rn − 1)/(r − 1) with first term a = 200 × 1.05 and r = 1.05:

  1. a = 200 × 1.05 = 210
  2. r = 1.05, n = 4
  3. S4 = 210 × (1.054 − 1) / (1.05 − 1)
  4. 1.054 ≈ 1.21550625
  5. S4 = 210 × (0.21550625) / 0.05 ≈ 210 × 4.310125 ≈ $905.13

Answer: Maria will have approximately $905.13 after 4 years.

Practice Questions

Question 1

James saves $150 at the end of every month. The bank offers a simple interest rate of 6% per year (0.5% per month). Assuming an arithmetic sequence of interest earned, how much interest will he have earned after 8 months if interest is calculated only on the principal each month?

Show Hint
Treat the monthly interest as an arithmetic sequence. First term a = 150 × 0.005 = 0.75, common difference d = 0.75. Use Sn = n/2 × [2a + (n−1)d].
Show Answer & Solution

Interest per month on $150 = 150 × 0.005 = $0.75.

After 8 months the total interest forms an arithmetic series with a = 0.75, d = 0.75, n = 8.

  1. S8 = 8/2 × [2(0.75) + (7)(0.75)] = 4 × [1.5 + 5.25] = 4 × 6.75 = 27

Total interest earned = $27.00

Question 2

A person invests $500 at the beginning of each year in an account that earns 4% compound interest per year. How much will the investment be worth after 5 years?

Show Hint
This is a geometric series. First term a = 500 × 1.04, common ratio r = 1.04, n = 5. Use Sn = a(rn − 1)/(r − 1).
Show Answer & Solution

a = 500 × 1.04 = 520, r = 1.04, n = 5

  1. 1.045 ≈ 1.2166529
  2. S5 = 520 × (1.2166529 − 1) / 0.04 = 520 × 0.2166529 / 0.04
  3. S5 ≈ 520 × 5.4163225 ≈ 2816.49

Value after 5 years ≈ $2,816.49

Population Growth

Worked Example

A town has a population of 12,000. It grows at a rate of 3% per year. What will the population be after 6 years? (Assume geometric growth.)

Pn = P0 × (1 + r)n

  1. P0 = 12 000, r = 0.03, n = 6
  2. P6 = 12 000 × (1.03)6
  3. 1.036 ≈ 1.194052
  4. P6 ≈ 12 000 × 1.194052 ≈ 14,328.63

Answer: The population will be approximately 14,329 people after 6 years.

Practice Questions

Question 3

A bacteria culture starts with 800 bacteria and doubles every 3 hours. How many bacteria will there be after 15 hours?

Show Hint
This is a geometric sequence. First term a = 800, common ratio r = 2. Number of terms: 15 ÷ 3 = 5 growth periods, so n = 6 terms (including the start). Use an = a × rn−1.
Show Answer & Solution

After 15 hours there have been 5 doublings → 6 terms in the sequence.

  1. a6 = 800 × 25 = 800 × 32 = 25 600

Number of bacteria = 25,600

Question 4

The population of a city was 50,000 in 2020. It increases by 2.5% each year. Estimate the population in 2028.

Show Hint
Geometric growth: Pn = 50 000 × (1.025)n where n = 8 (from 2020 to 2028).
Show Answer & Solution

n = 8 years, r = 1.025

  1. 1.0258 ≈ 1.2184
  2. P8 = 50 000 × 1.2184 ≈ 60 920

Estimated population in 2028 ≈ 60,920

Depreciation

Worked Example

A machine costs $25,000 and depreciates by 15% of its value each year (reducing-balance method). What is its value after 4 years?

Vn = V0 × (1 − r)n

  1. V0 = 25 000, r = 0.15, n = 4
  2. V4 = 25 000 × (0.85)4
  3. 0.854 ≈ 0.52200625
  4. V4 ≈ 25 000 × 0.52200625 ≈ $13,050.16

Answer: The machine is worth approximately $13,050 after 4 years.

Practice Questions

Question 5

A car bought for $18,000 depreciates by 12% of its value each year. Find its value after 5 years.

Show Hint
Use Vn = 18 000 × (0.88)5. Calculate 0.885 step by step or with a calculator.
Show Answer & Solution

V5 = 18 000 × (0.88)5

  1. 0.882 = 0.7744
  2. 0.884 = 0.77442 ≈ 0.599695
  3. 0.885 ≈ 0.599695 × 0.88 ≈ 0.527732
  4. V5 ≈ 18 000 × 0.527732 ≈ 9 499.18

Value after 5 years ≈ $9,499

Question 6

An office computer costs $4,500 and loses 20% of its value each year. After how many full years will its value first fall below $1,500?

Show Hint
Solve 4500 × (0.8)n < 1500. Simplify to (0.8)n < 1/3 ≈ 0.3333. Try successive values of n or take logarithms.
Show Answer & Solution

4500 × (0.8)n < 1500 → (0.8)n < 1500/4500 = 1/3 ≈ 0.3333

  1. n = 1: 0.80
  2. n = 2: 0.64
  3. n = 3: 0.512
  4. n = 4: 0.4096
  5. n = 5: 0.32768 < 0.3333

The value first falls below $1,500 after 5 years.
