Depreciation

Category: Financial Mathematics  •  Level: Intermediate  •  Duration: ≈ 25 min

Lesson Overview

Assets such as cars, machinery, computers and equipment lose value over time. This loss of value is called depreciation. Two common mathematical models are used:
  • Straight-line depreciation (arithmetic sequence) — the asset loses a fixed amount each year.
  • Reducing-balance depreciation (geometric sequence) — the asset loses a fixed percentage of its current value each year.
These models help businesses and individuals estimate the book value of assets for accounting, tax and resale purposes.

Learning Objectives

  • Distinguish between straight-line and reducing-balance depreciation.
  • Calculate the book value of an asset after n years using both methods.
  • Determine the rate of depreciation from given data.
  • Apply depreciation models to real business and personal scenarios.

Mathematical Examples

Example 1 – Straight-Line Depreciation (Arithmetic Sequence)

A machine costs $24 000 and depreciates by $2 000 each year.

Value after n years:
Vn = 24 000 − 2 000n
Value after 7 years:
V7 = 24 000 − 2 000 × 7 = 24 000 − 14 000 = $10 000
Example 2 – Reducing-Balance Method (Geometric Sequence)

A car is bought for $30 000 and depreciates at 15% of its value each year.

This is a geometric sequence with common ratio r = 0.85.
Vn = 30 000 × (0.85)n
Value after 4 years:
V4 = 30 000 × (0.85)4 ≈ 30 000 × 0.5220 = $15 660
Example 3 – Comparing the Two Methods

A laptop costs $1 200.
  • Straight-line: loses $200 each year.
  • Reducing-balance: loses 20% of its value each year.

Value after 3 years:
Straight-line:   V3 = 1 200 − 200 × 3 = $600
Reducing-balance:   V3 = 1 200 × (0.80)3 = 1 200 × 0.512 = $614.40

The reducing-balance method leaves a slightly higher book value after 3 years.

Practice Activity

Your Turn

1. A delivery van costs $28 000 and depreciates by $3 500 each year (straight-line).
  • Write the formula for its value after n years.
  • What is its book value after 5 years?

2. A computer costs $2 400 and depreciates at 25% per year (reducing-balance).
  • Write the general term for its value after n years.
  • Find its value after 3 years.
  • After how many full years will its value first drop below $800?

3. Explain in your own words one advantage and one disadvantage of using the reducing-balance method compared with the straight-line method.
