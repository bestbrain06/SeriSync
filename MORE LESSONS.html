Saving and Investment Plans

Key concepts:

  • Compound Interest: A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt) the exponential growth of money over time.
  • Annuities: Regular investments or payments at fixed intervals.
  • Risk-Return Trade-off: Higher potential returns come with higher risk.
  • Diversification: Spreading investments to manage risk.
  • Inflation: The erosion of purchasing power over time.

Learn how to build wealth through systematic saving, compound growth, and smart investment strategies.

lesson 2

Population Growth

Key concepts:

  • Exponential Growth: P(t) = P e^(rt) unlimited growth under ideal conditions.
  • Logistic Growth: Growth slows as population approaches carrying capacity (K).
  • Demographic Transition: The shift from high birth/death rates to low rates.
  • Doubling Time: Rule of 70 70 growth rate years to double.
  • Population Momentum: Continued growth even after fertility declines.

Explore how populations change over time and the mathematical models that describe them.

lesson 3

Depreciation

Key concepts:

  • Straight-Line: (Cost - Salvage) Useful Life equal annual expense.
  • Declining Balance: Fixed percentage applied to book value each year.
  • Double-Declining Balance: Twice the straight-line rate applied to book value.
  • Units of Production: Based on actual usage or output.
  • Sum of Years' Digits (SYD): Accelerated method using declining fractions.

Understand how assets lose value over time and the different accounting methods used to measure depreciation.
